Extended summary

The peak of the planet's strongest El Niño on record will reshape weather globally during the coming months, with sea surface temperatures almost 10°C warmer than normal near the Galapagos Islands and Peru. Some regions will experience extreme heat and drought while others face flooding, high humidity, or heavy snow. The phenomenon has already brought heavy rain, flooding, and extreme winds to America's west coast. In the UK, it could cause extra stormy weather and potentially blizzard conditions as colder Arctic air drifts into Europe and North America. The event may disrupt wildlife food chains and food supplies, potentially causing higher prices for rice, cocoa, and coffee. Forecasts for approximately 37,000 cities indicate San Francisco will be warmer, wetter, windier, more humid, and cloudier, while Washington D.C. faces wetter, windier, and more humid conditions with high chance of extreme rainfall.
