Ukrainian drones attacked an industrial zone in the southern Russian city of Volgograd, killing four people and injuring two. Falling debris caused localized fires at the Lukoil oil refinery, the largest in southern Russia, which processes around 14.8 million metric tons of crude oil annually. The facility had been attacked previously on October 2. Gasoline prices in Volgograd rose overnight by 50 kopeks per liter, prompting drivers to line up at stations. Russia’s Defense Ministry reported intercepting 1,198 Ukrainian drones over Russian regions and annexed Crimea overnight. The strike was at least the eighth on a Russian oil refinery since October 30. The attack came a day after U.S. envoys met with Russian and Ukrainian leaders in Moscow and Dublin.
