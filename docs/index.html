Extended summary

Panjagutta police station in Hyderabad is being developed as the country's first AI-powered police station under a pilot project directed by Telangana DGP C.V. Anand, scheduled for launch in the first week of December. Hyderabad Police Commissioner V.C. Sajjanar conducted a surprise inspection to review technological integration progress. The initiative will deploy artificial intelligence across all policing stages, from FIR registration and case diary maintenance to investigation reports and charge-sheet preparation. Police personnel are receiving special training to use the technology effectively. Based on the pilot's performance, the model may be expanded across Telangana.
