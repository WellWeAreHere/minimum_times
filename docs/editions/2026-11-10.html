Extended summary

Mumbai's civic body imposed a blanket ban on rockets and sky-flying firecrackers across the city from November 9 until 11:59 pm on November 25. The prohibitory order, issued by the Urban Disaster Management Authority, covers all public and private spaces including residential buildings, terraces, balconies, courtyards, parking lots, and slum areas within BMC limits. The ban was enacted after the Mumbai Fire Brigade received 142 fire calls on November 8 alone, with 137 attributed to firecrackers, and several major fires were reported at Dongri and Vidyavihar. The order cites the risk of burning remnants falling on rooftops and inflammable materials, potentially triggering fires in the densely populated city with high-rise buildings and congested slums. The Disaster Management Act, 2005 was invoked to enforce the preventive measure, while other firecrackers remain governed by existing laws. Zonal deputy commissioners, divisional assistant commissioners, and the fire brigade were directed to ensure implementation.
