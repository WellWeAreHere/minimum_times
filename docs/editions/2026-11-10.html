The Israeli military confirmed the shooting in Burqa, which is located near the city of Nablus. In a statement, the military said soldiers opened fire to prevent Palestinians from throwing stones at Israeli vehicles. “The forces responded with gunfire to neutralise the threat and several hits were confirmed,” it said.
