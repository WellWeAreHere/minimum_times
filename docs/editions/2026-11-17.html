Lindsay Clancy, the Massachusetts woman accused of murdering her three children, will face a retrial next year, a judge has said, after a jury deadlocked last time in a case that gripped the nation. The judge set a trial date for 29 March 2027, though the district attorney did not explicitly say what charges Clancy would face. Prosecutors said they wanted a jury trial, and the defence said they took that to mean Clancy, 36, would again face first-degree murder charges. Five-year-old Cora, Dawson, three, and eight-month-old Callan were strangled at the family home on 24 January 2023. The defence argues she had severe postpartum psychosis.
