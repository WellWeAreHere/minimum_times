Extended summary

The Union Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology launched the indigenously developed Chariot-1 Foundation Models under the IndiaAI Mission. Developed by Chariot Artificial Intelligence Pvt. Ltd., one of the first four teams selected for the mission, the suite includes a Large Language Model and a Text-to-Speech model pre-trained from scratch in India. The Chariot-1 LLM is an 11-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with four billion active parameters per token, available in variants of 10.7 billion, 1.7 billion and 745 million parameters. The TTS model generates speech in English and 10 Indian languages including Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi and Odia, with context-aware pronunciation of numbers, dates, currencies and other elements. It recorded over 97 percent verbalisation accuracy on the Async TTS Pronunciation Benchmark, a 7.60 percent character error rate in Hindi, and a 6.47 percent average across nine other Indian languages.
