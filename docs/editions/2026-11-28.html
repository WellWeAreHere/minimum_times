Research using Sentinel-1 satellite radar imagery reveals that some of Africa's largest coastal cities are sinking faster than previously estimated. Scientists mapped land movement beneath 20 African coastal cities, finding Lagos, Nigeria and Alexandria, Egypt sinking at average rates of 5mm and 6mm per year respectively. Human activities such as groundwater extraction drive this subsidence, which compounds sea level rise from climate change.
