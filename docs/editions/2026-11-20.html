Glenn Phillips scored 108 not out off 193 balls on Day 2 of the first Test against India at the Basin Reserve in Wellington, becoming the first player to score hundreds against India in all three formats in a single calendar year. He had earlier scored 106 in an ODI in Indore on January 18 and 107 off 49 balls in a T20I in Auckland on October 30. Phillips is only the second player after Rohit Sharma to achieve this against the same opponent in one year, and the first New Zealand batter to do so. He is the third player after Shane Watson and Shai Hope to score centuries in all three formats against India. Phillips shared a 216-run sixth-wicket partnership with Henry Nicholls, who scored a century on Day 1.

President Donald Trump announced a posthumous pardon for Pete Rose, baseball's career hits leader with 4,256 hits who died in 2024 at age 83. Rose pleaded guilty in 1990 to two counts of filing false tax returns, admitting he failed to report $354,968, and was sentenced to five months in prison and fined $50,000. He had agreed to a permanent ban from Major League Baseball in August 1989 after investigators found he bet on the Cincinnati Reds while playing and managing the team from 1985 to 1987. Last May, MLB Commissioner Rob Manfred restored the eligibility of deceased individuals on the permanently ineligible list, opening a pathway for Rose to be considered for the Baseball Hall of Fame as early as 2028. The pardon, issued more than two years after Rose's death, is largely symbolic and does not alter his Hall of Fame status.

Initially weighing 2,400 pounds, Fiona seemed fine at first, but her appetite declined days later. Zoo staff attempted numerous treatments including fluid therapy, enemas, a colonoscopy using a nine-foot scope borrowed from a nearby zoo, and a vibration plate from a local horse farm. Despite heroic efforts by veterinarians and keepers, including offering her coffee, Fiona's body could not overcome the blockage. Born prematurely at just 29 pounds, she had become a beloved attraction, and her death at age nine was considered premature as hippos typically live up to 50 years in human care.
