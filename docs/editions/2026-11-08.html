The Delhi High Court ordered the immediate removal of six AI-generated social media posts and videos portraying Chief Minister Rekha Gupta as a liquor vendor, hawker and delivery agent. Justice Manoj Jain granted interim relief in Gupta's defamation suit against the Aam Aadmi Party and others, covering five posts on X and one on Facebook. The court ruled that while public office invites scrutiny, political satire cannot be used to compromise an individual's dignity. The material allegedly depicted Gupta as head of a purported 'Har Har Rekha or Ghar Ghar Theka' scheme, with one video showing her and Deputy Chief Minister Parvesh Sahib Singh pouring liquor and ice cubes. The court directed platforms to remove the six URLs immediately, issued summons to the defendants, and asked them to file written statements within 30 days, with the matter scheduled for further consideration on December 4.
